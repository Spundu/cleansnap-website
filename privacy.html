Other Sharing

  • Between Users: Once a Cleaner is assigned to your booking, we share limited details between the Customer and Cleaner (name, phone number, property address and any access/pet/parking notes) to enable the service. Phone numbers are exchanged in both directions, and only while the job is live — from the moment a Cleaner is assigned until the job is completed or cancelled; after that you can still reach each other through in-app messages.
  • Offering a job to Cleaners: Before any Cleaner is assigned, we show your job to verified Cleaners whose service area covers it. We rely on our legitimate interest in keeping users safe and on our legal obligations under the Online Safety Act 2023 — not on your consent, so this is not something you can switch off while using the Platform. We do not use what you write to train anyone's AI models.
  • Reports and blocks: If you report a message, a review or a person, we record what you reported, the reason you chose, anything you wrote, and a copy of the reported content as it stood at that moment (so it can still be judged if the author later changes or deletes it). A member of our team reads it, and we keep what we decided and why. We never tell the person you reported who reported them. If you block someone, we record that so the two of you are never matched again — if you delete your account, that instruction survives, but anything you wrote about them does not.
  • Complaints about a job: If you raise a complaint — as a Customer about the clean, or as a Cleaner about the job — what you write and any photos you attach are shown to our team and to the other person involved, because we reach a decision by hearing both sides. Their reply is shown to you for the same reason. Neither of you is given the other's contact details through a complaint: it carries the account of what happened, not an address or a phone number.
  • If a Cleaner reports that you were not there: the report — the times, the Cleaner's location at those moments, the photograph of your entrance and your access instructions as they stood — is seen by our team, who decide it. If you dispute the charge through the in-app complaint or by email, we put what the report contains — the times, how far from your property the Cleaner was, and the photograph — in your complaint thread and send it to you by email, so that you can answer it; what you write in that complaint is shown to the Cleaner, as for any complaint. If the outcome changes because of what you told us, the Cleaner is told the reason.
  • HMRC (Cleaners only): As a digital platform we are required to report Cleaners' identity (name, date of birth, National Insurance number, address) and yearly earnings to HMRC under the Platform Operators (Due Diligence and Reporting Requirements) Regulations 2023, which implement the OECD Model Reporting Rules (the UK equivalent of the EU's "DAC7").

  • Business Transfer: If CleanSnap is acquired or merged, your data may be transferred to the new entity. We will notify you of any such transfer.

We never sell your personal data to third parties, and we do not use your photos or content to train AI models.

6. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you. You can generate a data export directly in the app.
  • Right to Rectification — Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data. You can update most data directly in the app.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten") — Request deletion of your data directly in the app or by contacting us. Subject to legal retention obligations (e.g., financial records for HMRC).
  • Right to Data Portability — Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format (the in-app export provides this as JSON).
  • Right to Restrict Processing — Request that we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Object — Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent — Where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@cleansnap.co.uk. We will respond within one calendar month.

If you are unsatisfied with our response, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO):

7. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously and implement appropriate technical and organisational measures, including:

  • AES-256-GCM encryption at rest for the most sensitive fields (National Insurance numbers, right-to-work share codes while a check is pending, two-factor secrets)
  • TLS/HTTPS for all data in transit (including our database connections)
  • Private file storage: photos and documents are never public and are only accessible through short-lived signed links
  • Data export files are encrypted at rest in storage
  • Bcrypt password hashing (12 rounds)
  • JWT-based authentication with short-lived tokens and rotation
  • Mandatory two-factor authentication for administrator accounts
  • Account lockout after repeated failed login attempts and rate limiting on all public endpoints
  • Automatic redaction of sensitive fields in application logs
  • Role-based access controls and per-user data isolation

No system is 100% secure. If we become aware of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights, we will notify the ICO within 72 hours as required by law, and we will inform you without undue delay where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to you.

9. Automated Decision-Making

We use AI to generate cleaning price quotes from the photos you upload (Google Gemini) and to check that your photos match the declared rooms (OpenAI). These are automated processes that produce a price estimate and photo-quality feedback. You are not obligated to accept any AI-generated quote, the assigned Cleaner may propose an adjusted price which you can accept or reject, and you can contact us if you believe a quote is inaccurate.

We also use automated ranking to suggest available Cleaners for a booking (based on factors such as distance, availability and ratings). Customers remain free to choose a different Cleaner.

Neither the quote nor the ranking produces legal effects or similarly significantly affects you within the meaning of UK GDPR Article 22. The decision described next does, and we treat it as one.

Two decisions we make by hand. Whether a report that a Customer was not at the property is upheld, and whether a Cleaner's right to work is confirmed, are each decided by a member of our team on the evidence described in section 2 — never automatically. When such a report is made, 50% of the booking total is charged to the Customer's card at once, before that decision, because a card authorisation does not last long enough to wait for it; the charge is refunded in full if the report is not upheld.

Cleaners: the automated decision that can stop new work being offered to you

This section applies only if you are a Cleaner. It describes a decision we make about you by automated means, with no person involved in making it, and we set it out here in full rather than argue about whether Article 22 covers it — it decides whether you are offered work, so we treat it as covered.

What we record. Every job you accept is recorded as an engagement: which booking it was, how you came by it, when you accepted it, when you started it, whether that start was inside the time slot the Customer agreed to, and how the engagement ended. From those records we keep two figures on your profile: the share of the jobs you complete, and the share you start within the agreed slot, each with the number of jobs it was counted over.

The logic. If either figure falls below 50% — each one counted only once there are at least three jobs to judge it on, so a single bad day never triggers it — the platform stops putting you forward. Jobs a Customer cancelled, jobs whose price you and the Customer did not agree, jobs where the Customer was not there, and jobs closed by us are excluded from the figures and do not count against you.

What happens. You stop appearing in Customer search results, you are no longer assigned to jobs automatically, and the job pool neither lists work to you nor lets you claim it. It is not a suspension and not a termination: your account, your login and every job you have already accepted are untouched, and carrying those out is what brings the figure back up and restores you automatically, with no application and no decision by us. We tell you when it happens and which of the two figures caused it.

Your rights over this decision. You can ask a person to look at it, put your side of it, and contest the outcome: write to support@cleansnap.co.uk. A member of our team will review the underlying records and can correct or set aside the result. You also have the right to be told the reasoning, which is the paragraph above, and to ask us to correct any engagement record you believe is wrong.

Why we are allowed to do it. Article 22(2)(a) UK GDPR: the decision is necessary for the performance of the contract between you and us. Section 9 of the Cleaner Agreement, which you accept before you can take work, states the same rule in the same terms. It uses no special category data and no profiling of anything other than the two figures described above.
