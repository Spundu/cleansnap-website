Cookie Policy

Version 1.2 — in force since 31 August 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember your preferences and understand how you use the site. Similar technologies include local storage and session storage used by mobile applications.

2. How We Use Cookies and Local Storage

CleanSnap operates primarily as a mobile application, and we set no cookies of our own: our servers never send a cookie to your browser. What the app keeps is stored on your device — in the secure keystore on a phone, and in your browser's local storage on the web — and is listed in full below. On the web, one third party sets its own cookies when you reach the payment step; see section 4.

Analytics

We do not currently use any analytics or crash-reporting service in the app or on the website, and we store nothing on your device for that purpose. Our own servers keep error logs so that we can fix faults; that happens on our side and stores nothing on your device. If this changes, we will update this policy before the change reaches you.

Marketing

We do not currently use marketing or advertising cookies. If this changes in the future, we will update this policy and request your consent before enabling any marketing cookies.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be set by third-party services that we use:

  • Stripe — Our payment processor may set cookies when you make a payment, for fraud prevention and security purposes.
  • Apple — If you sign in with Apple, Apple’s authentication service may set cookies. (Sign-in with Google is not enabled in the current release.)

We do not control third-party cookies. Please refer to those providers' cookie policies for more information.

5. Your Choices

You can manage cookies and local storage in several ways:

In the App

You can manage your notification preferences, and change your language, from the app settings. To remove everything listed in section 3, sign out — which deletes the three sign-in entries — or clear the app’s data from your device settings.

Browser Settings

If you access CleanSnap via a web browser, you can control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Block all cookies (note: this may prevent you from using the Platform)
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Allow cookies from specific sites only
  • Block third-party cookies

For instructions on managing cookies in your specific browser, visit your browser's help pages.

Device Settings

On mobile devices, you can manage app data and permissions through your device settings (Settings → Apps → CleanSnap).

6. Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you disable strictly necessary cookies or clear your local storage:

  • You will be signed out and need to log in again
  • Your preferences may be reset
  • Some features of the Platform may not function correctly

There are no analytics or marketing cookies to disable.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the app. The "Last updated" date below indicates when this policy was last revised.
