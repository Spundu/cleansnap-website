5. How the Platform Works

For Customers

  1. Request a Quote: Upload photos of the space to be cleaned and provide details about the service you need.
  2. Receive a Quote: Our AI-assisted system generates a price estimate based on the photos and service type.
  3. Accept and Book: If you accept the quote, your payment is authorised (held but not charged) and the job is offered to available Cleaners.
  4. Cleaning: A Cleaner accepts the job, travels to your location, and completes the clean.
  5. Payment: After completion, your payment is captured and the Cleaner is paid.

For Cleaners

  1. Register and Verify: Create an account, provide your professional details, and complete our verification process.
  2. Accept Jobs: Browse available jobs in your service area and accept those that suit your schedule.
  3. Complete the Clean: Arrive on time, perform the cleaning to professional standards, and mark the job as complete.
  4. Get Paid: Receive payment for completed jobs, minus the platform service fee.

Your Statutory Right to Cancel (Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013)

If you are a consumer, you have a statutory right to cancel a service contract made online within 14 days without giving any reason (the "cooling-off period"). For each booking, the cooling-off period ends 14 days after the day the booking is made.

  • Booking a clean within the cooling-off period: by placing a booking scheduled to take place within 14 days, you expressly request that the cleaning service is arranged and performed during the cooling-off period.
  • Loss of the right once the service is complete: once the cleaning service has been fully performed, you lose the statutory right to cancel that booking.
  • Cancelling before the service takes place: if you cancel during the cooling-off period before the service has been performed, you will be refunded. Where you cancel close to the scheduled time after expressly requesting performance, the cancellation fees below apply as compensation for the Cleaner's reserved time and costs reasonably incurred.
  • How to cancel: cancel in the app, or email support@cleansnap.co.uk with a clear statement that you wish to cancel, your name, and the booking reference. You may (but do not have to) use the model cancellation wording: "I hereby give notice that I cancel my contract for the supply of the following service: [booking reference]. Ordered on: [date]. Name and address of consumer: [...]. Date: [...]".

If you are not there

You agreed to make the property accessible to the Cleaner and to be available at the scheduled time (section 10). If the Cleaner arrives at the property inside the booked time slot and cannot get in — whether you were to let them in yourself, or the access you arranged (a key, a code) does not work — they tell you through the app that they have arrived (the app sends you a message and a notification), call you if you gave us a number, and wait at least 15 minutes. Answering the call is not the same as letting them in: if they still cannot get in when the wait is over, they may report through the app that you were not there, up to 15 minutes after the end of the slot. The app records the time and the Cleaner's location when they arrive and when they report, and a photograph of the entrance. A member of our team decides every report; the app never decides it.

Once a report is made the booking ends: the Cleaner is free to leave, and the clean will not go ahead that day even if you arrive afterwards. If you still want the clean, make a new booking. When the report is made we charge 50% of the booking total to the card held for the booking — the same fee as cancelling after the start time — and tell you at once by email (and by push notification, if you have them on); Stripe also sends you its receipt. We take it then, not after we have decided, because the card hold for a booking lasts only a few days; taking it is not the decision. A member of our team then reviews the report — where the Cleaner was and when, when they told you they had arrived, and the photograph — and aims to decide within 5 business days; we tell you the outcome by email. If we do not uphold the report, the charge is refunded in full, on the timescale in "Refunds" below.

You can give us your side through the app (open the booking and use "Raise a complaint") or at support@cleansnap.co.uk. We encourage you to do so within 14 days of being told, while the evidence is fresh, but this is not a deadline and does not limit your statutory rights. If you dispute the charge, we show you what the report contains: the times, how far from your property the Cleaner was, and the photograph. If you could not be there for a reason outside your control — an accident, a medical emergency, a bereavement — tell us: we refund the charge, and we will not ask you to prove a private matter. Our decision concludes our internal process; it does not affect your statutory rights as a consumer or your right to bring the matter before the courts (section 12).

As with any cancellation fee, half of what you pay goes to the Cleaner, who set the time aside and travelled to you — and if we excuse you for an emergency, we pay the Cleaner that share ourselves. The reverse case is free: if it is the Cleaner who has not arrived by the time your slot ends, you pay nothing. Once the slot has ended you can cancel in the app and the hold on your card is released in full — wait for the slot to end, because a slot is a window in which the Cleaner may arrive, and cancelling while it is still open counts as cancelling after the start time, with the fee in the table above. If the app does not offer you Cancel because the Cleaner had marked themselves as on their way, we close the booking ourselves within two days and release the hold, or email support@cleansnap.co.uk and we cancel it for you, with the hold released within the hour.

Changing a Booking

You can change the date or time slot of a booking in the app, and there is no fee for doing so. Once a Cleaner has been assigned, a booking can only be changed while cancelling it would still be free of charge — more than 48 hours before the scheduled start time, or more than 12 hours if your assigned Cleaner cancelled and the job was re-offered (see "Cleaner Cancellations" below). When you change a booking a Cleaner has accepted, we tell them the new date and time. They are not obliged to keep it: if it no longer suits them they may cancel, and the job is offered to other available Cleaners in the usual way.
